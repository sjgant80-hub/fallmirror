# FallMirror

A private reflection tool that runs entirely on your device. Nothing leaves your browser.

Talk to it about what's true right now. It won't judge or advise — it reflects back with careful questions across seven angles: what you're pulled between, what's holding you up, what's underneath, when this started, what could change, who else is in this, and what you already know. Over time it builds a private map of how you show up.

**Not for crisis.** If you're in acute distress or thinking of self-harm, this isn't the right tool — there's a big Crisis Help button that goes straight to human-answered lines.

Everything you type stays in your browser (IndexedDB). No accounts, no server, no tracking. Optional: enable in-browser AI for richer prompts (one-time ~4GB download that then works forever, still offline).
